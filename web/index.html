Click inside the game to use the keyboard. Esc pauses · C switches P1 camera · Enter switches P2 camera.

01

Choose your team.

Choose huskies, corgis, mastiffs, or twelve Chihuahuas. Then pick a sled. The chocolate one works about as well as you’d hope.

02

Share the keyboard.

Choose two-player mode for top-and-bottom split-screen. Player 1 gets WASD; Player 2 gets the arrows. Nobody gets the space bar.

03

A small experiment.

This is a game I’m making for fun and still working on. Your choices and best times stay in this browser. You can play on a phone too; multiplayer is two people sharing a keyboard.
