# Dogsled Kart

A cheerful racer with expressive dog teams, sliding sleds and playful contact.

The current game has four themed courses, three difficulty levels, four breeds with different team sizes and four sled styles. Play solo against three NPCs or in local split-screen with two humans and two NPCs. Each player can switch between chase and musher views.

Three-lap races include physical bumps, Golden Bone distractions, drift, boost, graduated off-track slowdown, recovery and a customizable garage. The courses have rolling elevation, smoother procedural models, textured surfaces and roadside characters: polar bears, arctic foxes, lemurs, toucans, swaying candy, gingerbread spectators, moving buses and pigeons.

Priorities for the next human playtest are two-player keyboard comfort, split-screen performance, musher visibility, corner recovery and NPC balance. Online multiplayer, mobile touch controls and web deployment remain future work.
