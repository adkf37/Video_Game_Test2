# Controls

Choose **Solo + 3 rivals** or **2 players + 2 rivals** in the garage. Local two-player mode uses a shared keyboard and top/bottom split-screen. Each player chooses their own dogs and sled.

| Action | Player 1 / solo | Player 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Accelerate / brake / steer | W / S / A / D | Arrow keys |
| Boost | F | Slash (/) |
| Drift | Shift | Period (.) |
| Throw Golden Bone | E | Comma (,) |
| Recover (+3 seconds) | R | Backspace |
| Chase / musher view | C | Enter |
| Pause both players | Esc | Esc |
| Sound | M (mute) | M (mute) |
| Volume down / up | - / + (or keypad) | - / + (or keypad) |

Space is unassigned to racing controls. Solo mode also accepts arrow steering. The separate controls in two-player mode keep each player's hands on their side of the keyboard. Actual simultaneous-key capacity depends on the keyboard's hardware.

Gamepads use left stick steering, RT throttle, LT brake, A boost, LB drift, X throw bone, Y recovery, RB camera and Start pause. Controller 1 belongs to Player 1 and controller 2 to Player 2. Physical gamepad validation remains outstanding.

Boost only works on the trail. Releasing boost recharges it; after running empty, release and press again. Drift preserves sideways momentum, so enter corners early and release to regain grip.

Collect a glowing Golden Bone on the trail, then press E (Player 1 / solo) or comma (Player 2) to throw it near the closest eligible opponent within 26 m. The HUD names the target. The bone lands off-track and lures that team for one second before their controls return. Targets get four more seconds of protection. You hold one bone at a time; pickups respawn after ten seconds. Throws without a nearby target keep the bone. NPCs collect and throw the same items. Sled contact still causes a physical bounce.

There are only three bones per track; drive within 1.9375 m to collect one. Watch the signs for crossing bears, buses, rolling snowballs and falling candy. Lollipops leave at least half the road clear. Move away from the orange coconut landing ring: a direct hit stops your dogs for one second. Hazards affect every racer and freeze when paused. Music plays throughout; M mutes all sound and -/+ change the volume.

The first forward finish-line crossing arms lap tracking. Complete the route and cross forward again to earn a lap. Missed markers never require turning back.

The first off-track cut is 6 m wide, with a gradual, mild slowdown. Over the next 8 m, the second cut slows you further. Boost is disabled in both cuts. Recovery returns to the nearest trail point, clears momentum and adds three seconds to that player's personal time without awarding skipped progress.

In local duels, a finished player stops driving while the other keeps racing. Results appear after both finish. Each player has their own lap counter, clock, boost, bone inventory and camera. Pause freezes both. Duels do not overwrite solo records.

In solo mode, the whole table resolves when you finish. Remaining NPC times are estimates (marked `est.`), calculated from their pace and remaining distance. No waiting for their clocks to finish.
